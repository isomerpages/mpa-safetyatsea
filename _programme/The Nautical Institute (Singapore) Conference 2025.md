  <h3>The Nautical Institute (Singapore) Conference 2025</h3>
	<p>Venue: Furama City Centre, Singapore<br>
Register Here: <a href="https://reg.eventnook.com/event/nautinstsg2025">https://reg.eventnook.com/event/nautinstsg2025</a></p>
	<p>The Nautical Institute (Singapore) Conference 2025 returns with a timely and thought-provoking theme — <b>Maritime 4.0: Embracing Digitalisation, Sustainability, and Wellbeing</b>.</p>
<p>As the maritime industry undergoes rapid transformation, this year’s conference will explore how innovation, environmental responsibility, and crew welfare are redefining the future of seafaring. The one-day event will feature engaging panel discussions, expert debates, and meaningful networking opportunities with maritime professionals from across the region.</p>

<p>Conference Highlights:
</p><ul>
<li>Navigation<br>
A lively debate-style session will tackle the evolution of navigation, focusing on key issues such as the rise of autonomous ships, implementation of ECDIS S-100, and AI in navigational systems. The legal and ethical implications of pilotage in a digital world will also be critically examined.</li>

<li>Sustainability<br>
Explore the challenges and opportunities of going green in maritime. Topics include the risks of transitioning to future fuels, the training gaps for seafarers, the growth of electric harbour ferries, and the potential role of nuclear energy.</li>

<li>Seafarer Wellbeing<br>
Gain insights into the human side of shipping. Discussions will revolve around gender equality, cadet training standards, mentoring, and how the industry can better support mental health and access to wellbeing services for all seafarers.</li>
</ul><p></p>
