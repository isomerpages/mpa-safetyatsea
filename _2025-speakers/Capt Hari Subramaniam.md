<h4>Capt Hari Subramaniam</h4>
<p>Deputy Chairman, National Maritime Safety at Sea Council<br>
Chairperson, The Nautical Institute (Singapore)<br>
Regional Head – Business Relations,<br>
The Shipowners’ P&amp;I Club</p>
<p>After 17 years of sailing Hari came ashore and headed the Commercial and Operations department of a reputable shipping company. Hari then moved on to help start-up the operations of a leading offshore company which owned and operated several state-of-the-art AHTSVs and PSVs. </p><p>
Hari joined The Shipowners’ Club in Sep 2009 overseeing the development and growth of the Loss Prevention Department in 2019, Hari then assumed a larger role as Regional Head – Business relations whereby he enhances deeper engagement with key stakeholders to improve safety awareness and risk management. In recognition of his efforts, Hari was awarded the “Outstanding Individual Contribution to Safety at Sea” by the MPA Singapore in Dec 2020. Hari also contributes to the industry as Deputy Chairman of the MPA's National Maritime Safety at Sea Council (NMSSC) as well as the MPA- Harbour Craft Safety working group. Hari is the Chairperson of the Nautical Institute (Singapore) and also a Fellow of the Nautical Institutes’ Main Governing Council.</p><p>
Hari has an MBA degree international management. He has a PG Diploma in Marine Insurance with Lloyd’s Maritime Academy and also a PG Diploma in Maritime Energy from World Maritime University (WMU).
 </p>
