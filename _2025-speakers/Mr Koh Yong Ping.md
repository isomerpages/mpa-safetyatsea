<h4>Mr Koh Yong Ping</h4>
<p>Chief Executive<br>
Bureau Veritas Marine (Singapore) </p>
<p>Mr Koh Yong Ping is the Chief Executive of Bureau Veritas Marine (Singapore).</p><p>
Trained as a Marine Engineer at the University of Newcastle-upon-Tyne, UK, he spent 9 years in the Republic of Singapore Navy, dealing with various projects and programs, including surface and subsurface technologies. Subsequently, he took up a post-graduate degree in Safety, Health, and Environmental Engineering from the National University of Singapore (NUS).</p><p>
In his professional capacities, he has contributed to various roles in the industry. Serving as a Council member of the Singapore Shipping Association (SSA), he chaired the Technical Committee and served as a focal point for industry to feedback on local policies as well as IMO regulations through MPA. During COVID-19, he was the Chair of an international tripartite group (SG-STAR TF) that
helped to identify and develop procedures and practices to promote seafarers’ health, crew-change, and vaccination (SEAVAX).</p><p>
Currently, he serves as a member of the Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT) Industry Advisory Council, MINDEF’s Requisition of Compensation Board, Singapore Standards’ M&amp;O Standards Technical Committee, Workplace Safety and Health (MI) Committee and Co-President of the French Chamber of commerce in Singapore Maritime and Offshore Committee.
 </p>
