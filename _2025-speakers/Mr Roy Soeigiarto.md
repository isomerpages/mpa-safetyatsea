<h4>Mr Roy Soeigiarto</h4> 
<p>Managing Director<br> Kairos Strategic</p>
<p>Mr Roy Soeigiarto is a business owner specializing in the distribution of polyethylene rotomold boats, small inflatable boats and rigid hull inflatable boats (RHIBs) in Asia.</p><p>

These crafts play critical roles in tourism, safety, security and emergency applications.</p><p>

Roy’s boating journey began at 18 when he earned his Powered Pleasure Craft Driving License (PPCDL) when it was first implemented and he has been a avid boater and also owns and manages a fleet of boats that supports safety, security &amp; tourism.</p><p>

A dedicated family man, Roy values time spent with his wife and four children. In his leisure, he enjoys boating, fishing, diving and traveling.
 </p>
