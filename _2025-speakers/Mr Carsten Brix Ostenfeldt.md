	<h4>Mr Carsten Brix Ostenfeldt</h4>
<p>Chief Operating Officer<br>Anglo-Eastern Ship Management</p>
<p>Mr Carsten Ostenfeldt is the COO of Anglo-Eastern Univan Group, one of the world's foremost ship management companies, headquartered in Hong Kong, with offices across Asia, Europe, and the Americas.</p><p>
As COO, Carsten has oversight of over 750 vessels, including bulk carriers, container ships, tankers, and cruise ships managed by the group's Hong Kong, Singapore, Athens, and Miami offices.</p><p>
Complementing his career in ship management is a strong and extensive background in marine engineering and technical services, where he held various positions over 15 years, from supervising new engines in production to heading the group's technical services and global service divisions.</p><p>
Carsten, who also holds an Executive MBA in Shipping &amp; Logistics ("Blue MBA") from Copenhagen Business School, is currently a regional committee member of Class NK in Hong Kong and an Executive Committee and Council Member of INTERTANKO.
 </p>
