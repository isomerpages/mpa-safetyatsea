<h4>Mr Daniel Ong</h4>
<p>HSEQ Manager, marinEV<br>
Yinson GreenTech</p>
<p>Mr Daniel Ong is a seasoned HSEQ professional with over 10 years of experience in the marine and offshore oil and gas sector. He began his career as a seafarer aboard subsea operation vessels, gaining firsthand insight into vessel operations, crew dynamics, and the practical application of safety management systems. This operational exposure shaped his risk-based, pragmatic approach to safety management, enabling him to deliver effective and operationally grounded safety programs.</p><p>

Building on this foundation, Daniel now applies his expertise to enhance operational and environmental safety in the harbour craft sector. He champions the adoption of electrification and emerging maritime technologies as part of a broader strategy to improve safety performance and support sustainable vessel operations.
 </p>
