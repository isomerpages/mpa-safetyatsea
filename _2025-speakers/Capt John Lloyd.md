<h4>Capt John Lloyd</h4>
<p>Chief Executive<br>
The Nautical Institute</p>
<p>Captain John Lloyd has been Chief Executive of The Nautical Institute since May 2017 having originally joined the Institute in 2015 as Chief Operating Officer. </p><p>
Previously he was Professor, Maritime Training at the Australian Maritime College (AMC).  In 2014 he inaugurated a new maritime college in Angola developed in with the City of Glasgow College and Sonangol Shipping.</p><p>
Prior to this he held appointments as: Chief Executive Officer of the Vanuatu Maritime College and senior posts at Flagship Training Limited and Warsash Maritime Centre.</p><p>
John is a Master Mariner and served as a Marine Pilot in Namibia from 1994 – 1995.  He is a Master of Business Administration, a Freeman of the (UK) Honourable Company of Master Mariners, a Younger Brother of Trinity House and a Chartered Master Mariner.</p><p>
In September 2023, John  was awarded the (UK) Merchant Navy Medal for Meritorious Service for his contribution to maritime training and education.
