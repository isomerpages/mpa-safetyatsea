<h4>Mr Ashish Mediratta</h4>
<p>Head of Crewing<br>
Maersk</p>
<p>Mr Ashish Mediratta is Head of Crewing for Maersk, responsible for management of Crew on board Maersk Owned Vessels. He is involved in Strategy related to development of Seafarer for the future,  Manning of Maersk vessels. He possesses extensive experience in Ship Management in various departments in Maersk eg. Fleet Manager, Heading Vessel Performance (Fuel Efficiency) .</p><p>

A firm believer for "Care for our people, Diversity at Sea" while having a data-driven approach and supporting the digital journey. He is a proud ex-Seafarer and Master Mariner having sailed on various types of Vessels during a memorable Sea Career.
 </p>
