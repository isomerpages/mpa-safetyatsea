<h4>Mr Adrian Dunlop</h4>
<p>Vessel Operations Lead (Director of Owning Companies)<br>
Ocean Infinity</p>
<p>Mr Adrian Dunlop started his career offshore overseeing complex deep sea salvage operations predominantly with ROV operations.&nbsp; This went on to overseeing challenging mobilizations and vessel conversions to set up novel technology to map the seabed faster than any other company at the time.&nbsp; From here, he went into vessel management overseeing all operations involved in seabed mapping and large projects with some of the big oil majors and some high-profile search and salvage projects.&nbsp; At Ocean Infinity, he undertook an ambitious project of new build vessels that were remote ready and lean manned and eventually he got the position to head up the fleet of company owned vessels from everything including new build to into service and having them commercially ready.&nbsp; He has been with Ocean Infinity since its start in 2017.
 </p>
